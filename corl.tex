\begin{abstract}
  Quadruped locomotion in unstructured environments remains a
  significant challenge. This paper proposes a novel method for
  generating non-gaited, dynamic footstep plans using \textit{GaitNet}. By directly
  exploring the output space through projected gradient ascent,
  GaitNet optimizes footstep coordinates over its own reward
  surface. We formulate the problem with 4 parallel tracks and 4
  steps of projected gradient ascent per foot. Our system is
  evaluated in simulation and a physical Unitree Go1 robot navigating complex
  terrain, demonstrating improvements a state-of-the-art model
  predictive control baseline.
\end{abstract}
