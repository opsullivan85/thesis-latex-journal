\section{Sweep on OCS2}
\label{app:sweep}

\looseness=-1 The sweep (Fig.~\ref{fig:sweep-main}) extends the benchmark to
eight nominal void fractions from 0 to \corlOcsMaxVoidPct\% (above
\corlMaxVoidPct\%, beyond the training curriculum) and five speeds from
\corlOcsSpeedMin{} to \corlOcsSpeedMax\,m/s (the highest above the
training speeds), \verify{on Isaac's nominal scale, on which the
benchmark's \corlBenchVoidLow, \corlBenchVoidMid, and
\corlBenchVoidHigh\% are \corlBenchIsaacVoidLow,
\corlBenchIsaacVoidMid, and \corlBenchIsaacVoidHigh\%}. It uses
\verify{the benchmark's checkpoints (five training seeds per learned
method, learned values their means)} and tiles on
worlds other than the benchmark's, shared by all methods, and a
stricter criterion: a trial succeeds only if the robot reaches the end
of a \corlOcsTrackM\,m track within \corlOcsTimeFactor{} times the time
needed at the commanded speed, which also fails a robot that stalls.
A method's rate here therefore differs from its rate
in Table~\ref{tab:results}. The two panels are separate run sets with
\corlOcsTrialsPerPoint{} trials per point. In panel~(a), each void
fraction's trials are spread over the five speeds, and in panel~(b),
each speed's trials are 10 per void fraction. Panel~(a) shows GaitNet,
OCS2's own trot, GaitNet-Single, and Per-candidate + IFM, and panel~(b)
GaitNet, GaitNet-Single, and the trot. For GaitNet-Diagonal, only the
overall rate is available.

\looseness=-1 \verify{Overall, GaitNet succeeded in \corlSweepFull\%
against the trot's \corlSweepTrot\%. The two were equal at 0 and 10\%
voids, and from 30\% voids on GaitNet led by 15 to 42 points. At 50,
60, and \corlOcsMaxVoidPct\% voids it succeeded in 80, 60, and 50\%
against the trot's 38, 30, and 12\%, and at \corlOcsSpeedMax\,m/s in 66
against 51\%. GaitNet-Single succeeded in \corlSweepSingle\%,
Per-candidate + IFM in \corlSweepMlp\%, and GaitNet-Diagonal in
\corlSweepDiag\%.}
