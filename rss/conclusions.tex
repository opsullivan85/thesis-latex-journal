\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% 2026-11-13 refocus: small action space, emergent acyclic gait.

\looseness=-1 With one of at most \corlTotalCandidates{} choices per
tick, CADENCE stepped for a model-based controller without a gait
schedule, and its emergent gait was acyclic yet paired diagonal legs. On
OCS2, it beat the controller's own cyclic gaits, most on dense voids,
with only its IFM refitted. These results are from simulation, against
stock untuned gaits, with acyclicity measured on one earlier run, and
the mixer, history, and timing alone are not isolated. Next, we will
test CADENCE on hardware and other controllers, and measure how few
logged steps the refit needs \citep{xie_fada_2026}.
