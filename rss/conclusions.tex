\section{Conclusion and Limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% 2026-11-04 flow rewrite: plainer words, less repetition; same facts, numbers and \verify items.

\looseness=-1 Trained on a convex MPC, GaitNet ran frozen on OCS2 with
only its IFM refitted. On the benchmark, it beat OCS2's own trot, most
on the densest voids, and kept most of its success from the training
controller, but without the IFM it failed in most trials on both
controllers. In successful trials, it kept the trot's
diagonal pairing but chose its own timing. \emph{Policies:}
we report means over \corlNumTrainSeeds{} training seeds per method, without
their spread, so differences of a few points between versions may not
hold. \emph{Controllers:}
we tested one pair, in simulation (GaitNet has not yet run on a
Unitree Go1), and have not compared the refitted IFM with the training
controller's IFM or a constant or speed-dependent offset per leg. \emph{Terrain:} flat
ground with square voids of one size, at speeds up to
\corlOcsSpeedMax\,m/s; the benchmark's success criterion does not
penalize a robot that stops halfway, unlike the sweep's.
\emph{Perception:} depth images were noise-free, and we have not
measured landing errors next to voids (Sec.~\ref{subsec:idm-theory}). Future work will test whether fewer
logged steps \citep{xie_fada_2026} or relogging with the deployed IFM
\citep{christiano_transfer_2016} cheapens the refit.
