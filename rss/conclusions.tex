\looseness=-1 Trained on a convex MPC, GaitNet ran frozen on OCS2 with
only its IFM refitted and succeeded in \corlBenchFull\% of benchmark
trials, against \corlBenchTrot\% and \corlBenchStaticWalk\% for OCS2's
own trot and static walk, and in at most \corlBenchCtrlMax\% without the
refit. These results are from simulation, with one pair of
controllers, square voids of one size on flat ground, noise-free depth
images, and speeds up to \corlOcsSpeedMax\,m/s. Next, we will deploy
GaitNet on hardware, test other controllers, and measure how few logged
steps the refit needs \citep{xie_fada_2026}.
