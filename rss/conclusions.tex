\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% 2026-11-04 flow rewrite: plainer words, less repetition; same facts, numbers and \verify items.

\looseness=-1 Trained on a convex MPC, GaitNet ran frozen on OCS2 with
only its IFM refitted and succeeded in \corlBenchFull\% of benchmark
trials, against \corlBenchTrot\% for OCS2's own trot and
\corlBenchStaticWalk\% for its static walk, keeping most of its
\corlBenchIsaacIdm\% success on the training controller. With the
training controller's IFM, a constant offset per leg, or no IFM, it
succeeded in at most \corlBenchTrainIfm\%, so the refit carried the
transfer. These results come from simulation, with one pair of
controllers, square voids of one size on flat ground, noise-free depth
images, and speeds up to \corlOcsSpeedMax\,m/s. Future work will deploy
GaitNet on a Unitree Go1, move it between further controllers, and test
how few logged steps the refit needs \citep{xie_fada_2026} and whether
relogging with the deployed IFM \citep{christiano_transfer_2016} improves
its coverage of the deployed footholds.
