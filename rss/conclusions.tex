\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
% 2026-11-04 flow rewrite: plainer words, less repetition; same facts, numbers and \verify items.

\looseness=-1 Trained on a convex MPC, GaitNet ran frozen on OCS2 with
only its IFM refitted and succeeded more often than OCS2's own trot and
static walk, and far less often without the refit. These results are from simulation, with one pair of
controllers, stock gaits as the only non-learned baselines, one void
size on flat ground, noise-free depth, speeds up to
\corlOcsSpeedMax\,m/s, and no test that isolates the mixer or the
history. Next, we will deploy GaitNet on hardware, test other
controllers, and measure how few logged steps the refit needs
\citep{xie_fada_2026}.
